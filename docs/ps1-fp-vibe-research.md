# PS1 First-Person 3D Vibe Research

Research date: 2026-05-30

## Games To Study

These are useful references for modern games that successfully pull off a first-person, 3D, PS1-adjacent look.

- [Lunacid](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1745510/Lunacid/) - First-person dungeon crawler with obvious King's Field lineage.
- [Dread Delusion](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1574240/Dread_Delusion/) - First-person open-world RPG with a retro 3D aesthetic.
- [Chasing Static](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1319420/Chasing_Static/) - First-person Welsh sci-fi horror with crunchy PS1-like low-poly presentation.
- [Paratopic](https://store.steampowered.com/app/897030/Paratopic/) - Short first-person retro-3D horror; useful for cuts, pacing, and grimy texture work.
- [Fatum Betula](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1281270/Fatum_Betula/) - Surreal first-person exploration; useful for low-res rendering, vertex wobble, and texture flaws.
- [The Heilwald Loophole](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1697720/The_Heilwald_Loophole) - First-person hospital horror with strong PS1 horror character and environment language.
- [A Place, Forbidden](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1333920/A_Place_Forbidden/) - Free first-person lo-fi horror-puzzle, originally from Haunted PS1 Demo Disc 2020.
- [THE ENIGMA MACHINE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/977810/THE_ENIGMA_MACHINE/) - First-person sci-fi horror with glitchy mid-90s 3D/VHS presentation.
- [MOTHERED](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1830720/MOTHERED__A_ROLEPLAYING_HORROR_GAME/) - First-person domestic PS1/VHS horror.
- [[ECHOSTASIS]](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1558000/ECHOSTASIS/) - First-person experimental cyber-horror; more abstract, but visually useful.
- [BABBDI](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2240530/BABBDI/) - Free first-person exploration in a huge low-poly city; strong spatial design reference.
- [Lorn's Lure](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1417930/Lorns_Lure/) - First-person platformer with modernized retro 3D graphics.
- [THRESHOLD](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2580020/THRESHOLD/) - First-person retro horror with a tight setting and strong atmosphere.
- [Iron Lung](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1846170/Iron_Lung/) - First-person minimal 3D horror; useful as a study in constraint.
- [Lost in Vivo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/963710/Lost_in_Vivo/) - First-person Silent Hill-ish horror; less pure PS1, but useful for claustrophobic pacing.

## Real PS1 Assets And Textures

Important: real PS1 rips are copyrighted. Use these for study, reference, greybox experiments, or private prototypes. Do not ship a commercial game with ripped PS1 assets unless you have rights.

- [The Models Resource - PlayStation](https://models.spriters-resource.com/playstation/) - Broad PS1 model archive.
- [The Textures Resource - PlayStation](https://textures.spriters-resource.com/playstation/) - Broad PS1 texture archive.
- [King's Field III models](https://models.spriters-resource.com/playstation/kingsfieldiii/) - Especially useful for this target: first-person fantasy/horror, weapons, enemies, items, doors, graves, furniture, torches, ruins, and props.
- [LSD: Dream Emulator textures](https://textures.spriters-resource.com/playstation/lsddreamemulator/) - First-person dream/horror texture sets across surreal location moods.
- [Mega Man Legends models](https://models.spriters-resource.com/playstation/megamanlegends/) - Useful for readable low-poly NPCs, vehicles, props, and chunky silhouettes.
- [Metal Gear Solid models](https://models.spriters-resource.com/playstation/mgs/) - Small set, but useful for studying face and cloth texture economy.
- [Resident Evil 2 textures](https://textures.spriters-resource.com/playstation/resi2/asset/365673/) - Character texture sheets; useful for how PS1-era characters fake facial and clothing detail density.

## Extraction And Format Tools

- [PSXPREV](https://rickomax.github.io/psxprev/) - Open-source PS1 model/texture viewer and extractor. Supports formats like TMD, HMD, PMD, and TIM; exports models to OBJ/DAE/glTF2 and textures to PNG.
- [PSXPREV GitHub](https://github.com/rickomax/psxprev) - Technical details and releases.
- [DuckStation 3D Screenshot fork](https://github.com/scurest/duckstation-3D-Screenshot) - Exports rendered PS1 scenes; useful for study, but results can vary by game.
- [TIM format notes](https://open-ribbon.github.io/documentation/file-types/tim/) - Explanation of real PS1 texture/image format constraints.

- [PSX Silent Shelter bunker pack](https://applethecode.itch.io/psx-style-the-silent-shelter-low-poly-asset-pack) - Paid, royalty-free bunker-themed asset pack.

## Practical Takeaway

Study King's Field III, LSD: Dream Emulator, Fatum Betula, and Lunacid first. That gives the cleanest line from real PS1 constraints to modern first-person PSX execution.
